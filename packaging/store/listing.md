## 中文（zh-CN）

### 产品名称
声麦 Studio Pro

### 简短描述（256 字符以内）
录音、修音、编曲、混音与导出，全部在本机完成的一体化音频工作站。支持按字切分、逐块修音、个人音色补音、AI 伴奏、调音助手与分轨导出。

### 详细描述（10,000 字符以内）
声麦 Studio Pro 是一套在本机完成的音频工作站：录音、修音、编曲、混音与导出在同一处做完。人声进来后切成按字或按音节的块，逐块改音高与长度，再叠和声、配伴奏、上效果器链，最后按响度标准导出成品。

**修音与编辑**

- **按字切分**：依据元音核定位每个字与音节的边界，逐块调整音高、长度或删除；原录音只读，改坏可撤销。
- **音高曲线**：半音与音分微调，逐块处理，不改变原始录音。
- **一键调音**：本机测量响度、峰值、频段与音高偏差，输出可勾选的调整方案，不自动修改工程。
- **跟原唱**：导入原唱人声作为参照，分析音高差异并给出修正方案，确认后才修改。
- **个人音色补音**：用你自己录音训练的音色模型，把一段音延长或缩短（0.3×–8×）得更接近本人音色。模型默认在本机运行，可试听、可撤销。

**编曲与混音**

- **和声与加厚**：按音程生成和声轨，左右加厚，做成叠唱效果。
- **多轨工程**：BPM 网格、吸附与量化、变调、每轨音量与声像、混音台、逐轨插件链。
- **内置效果器**：滤波、三段 EQ、压缩、限幅、失真、合唱、镶边、颤音、位深压缩，逐轨插入。
- **VST3 插件**：离线渲染并冻结轨道，导出时按相同顺序重算。
- **伴奏与鼓机**：导入伴奏，或用鼓组在卷帘里点格子编写节奏。

**生成与输出**

- **AI 伴奏**：用文字描述风格与歌词，由第三方音乐生成平台产出伴奏或整首歌，生成后自动拆分为人声与伴奏两条轨。只发送文字，不发送录音。
- **调音助手**：用一句话描述听感（例如「人声太干」「低频太多」），由语言模型换算为具体参数改动，逐条列出，勾选后才应用，一次撤销可全部还原。只发送参数与这句话。
- **母带与导出**：按目标响度（LUFS）导出 WAV / MP3，可写标签与封面；也可把每条轨道分别导出并打包为 ZIP。
- **界面语言**：简体中文、繁體中文、English、日本語、한국어，随时切换。

**关于第三方服务**

AI 伴奏与调音助手需要你在软件内自行配置相应平台与密钥，费用由你与该平台直接结算；软件本身不含账号，也不代为充值。不配置也能正常使用其余全部功能。

**隐私**

录音、工程与导出文件只保存在本机。软件不含账号、不含统计上报。个人音色补音默认在本机运行；仅在你自行配置了云端地址时，才会把选中的那一段音频发送到你配置的服务器。首次启用语音识别时，从项目的 GitHub 发布页下载识别模型文件。

**运行要求**

Windows 10 1809 或更高版本（64 位）、4 GB 内存、500 MB 可用磁盘空间、可用麦克风。界面使用系统自带的 WebView2 运行时。

## English (en-US)

### Product name
声麦 Studio Pro

### Short description (max 256 characters)
An integrated audio workstation for recording, tuning, arranging, mixing and export — all processed locally on your own PC. Word-level vocal splitting, personal voice timbre, AI accompaniment and a mixing assistant.

### Description (max 10,000 characters)
声麦 Studio Pro is an integrated audio workstation that runs entirely on your own PC. Recording, tuning, arranging, mixing and export happen in one place: audio comes in, is split into word- or syllable-sized blocks for block-level pitch and length editing, then gains harmonies, backing tracks and a per-track plug-in chain before being exported to a target loudness standard.

**Tuning and editing**

- **Word and syllable splitting** — block boundaries are located from vowel nuclei, so each block follows what was actually sung. Adjust pitch, change length, or delete a block; the original recording is read-only and every edit can be undone.
- **Pitch curve** — edit the curve and nudge by semitones and cents, block by block.
- **One-click tuning** — measure loudness, peaks, frequency balance and pitch deviation locally and get a tickable list of adjustments. Nothing is changed automatically.
- **Reference-vocal matching** — analyse a reference vocal, get a correction plan, and apply it only after confirming.
- **Personal voice timbre** — an RVC model trained on your own recordings makes stretched notes sound like you. It runs on your machine by default; previewable and undoable.

**Arranging and mixing**

- **Harmonies and doubling** — generate harmony tracks by interval and widen with left/right doubles.
- **Multitrack projects** — tempo grid, snapping and quantize, transposition, per-track volume and pan, mixer, per-track plug-in chains.
- **Built-in effects** — filter, three-band EQ, compressor, limiter, distortion, chorus, flanger, tremolo, bit-crush, inserted per track.
- **VST3 plug-ins** — rendered offline with track freeze; exports recalculate in the same order.
- **Backing tracks and drums** — import backing audio, or program a drum kit on the piano roll.

**Generation and output**

- **AI accompaniment** — describe a style and lyrics in words and a third-party music platform produces a backing track or a full song, split into vocal and backing tracks. Text only; no audio is sent.
- **Mixing assistant** — describe what you hear in one sentence ("the vocal is too dry") and a language model turns it into concrete parameter changes, listed one by one and applied only after you tick them. Only parameters and that sentence are sent.
- **Mastering and export** — export WAV / MP3 to a loudness target, with tags and cover art; or export every track separately and pack them into a ZIP.
- **Interface languages** — Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, switchable at any time.

**Third-party services**

AI accompaniment and the mixing assistant require a platform account and key that you configure yourself inside the app; billing is between you and that platform. The app has no account system and never tops up on your behalf. Every other feature works without configuring anything.

**Privacy**

Recordings, projects, and exports stay on your computer. There are no accounts and no analytics. Personal voice timbre runs locally by default; only if you configure a cloud address is the selected audio clip sent to the server you configured. The speech-recognition model is downloaded from the project's GitHub release page the first time recognition is enabled.

**Requirements**

Windows 10 version 1809 or later (64-bit), 4 GB RAM, 500 MB free disk space, and a microphone. The interface uses the WebView2 runtime included with Windows.
